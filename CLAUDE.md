## Brand Voice

- Tone: warm, personal, trustworthy — the website emphasizes "your satisfaction is our main priority" and personalized, hassle-free service. Match that: helpful and inviting, not aggressive or discount-shouty.
- Language: English by default. Mix in Nepali phrases naturally where it fits domestic-audience content (place names, greetings, cultural references) — ask Krishna if unsure on tone for Nepali-heavy posts.
- Things to avoid: no exaggerated claims, no fake urgency ("only 2 spots left!"), no emoji-per-line spam. Keep it a couple of relevant emojis at most, if any.
- Reference proof points (use sparingly, not every post): 15+ years experience, 15k+ happy travelers, 650+ places visited — good for credibility posts, not needed on every package post.
- Example customer sentiment to echo in tone (not to quote directly): personalized service, insider local knowledge, stress-free planning, attention to detail.

## Posting Rules

- Frequency: 3x/week — Monday, Wednesday, Friday
- Preferred posting time: Evening, 5:00–7:00 PM NPT (aim for 6:00 PM NPT)
- Content mix (current focus = domestic):
  - 50% domestic package spotlights (specific destinations — hills, heritage sites, plains across Nepal)
  - 20% travel tips / practical info for domestic trips (best season, what to pack, hidden gems)
  - 15% social proof (traveler stories, milestones — 15k+ travelers etc.)
  - 15% behind-the-scenes / company culture / team
- Always include: a clear call-to-action (WhatsApp number or "DM us" / "visit btdpl.com")
- Never post about: unconfirmed pricing, unconfirmed departure dates, comparisons to named competitors
